01.2 - 12(twelve) Terms You Need Now

  • Chatbot: An AI you talk to. It answers, then waits for the next message.
  • Agent: An AI that can work through a task. It plans, uses tools, checks the result, and adjusts.
  • Worker: Our name for an agent with a standing job. A worker has a clear responsibility, a trigger, tools, limits, and a way to report back.
