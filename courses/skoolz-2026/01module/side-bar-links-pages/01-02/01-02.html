01.2 - 12(twelve) Terms You Need Now

  • Chatbot: An AI you talk to. It answers, then waits for the next message.
  • Agent: An AI that can work through a task. It plans, uses tools, checks the result, and adjusts.
  • Worker: Our name for an agent with a standing job. A worker has a clear responsibility, a trigger, tools, limits, and a way to report back.

Terms

Model

  • What it means: the trained AI engine that reads your request and produces a response.
  • Why it matters: different models have different strengths, costs, speed, and reliability.
  • Where it shows up: when you choose what your worker thinks with. Examples include Claude, GPT, Gemini, and other model providers.

Prompt

  • What it means: the instruction and background you give the model.
  • Why it matters: a vague prompt creates vague work. A clear prompt gives the worker a job, standards, and boundaries.
  • Where it shows up: when you write directions like, "Summarize today's inbox and flag anything that needs me."
